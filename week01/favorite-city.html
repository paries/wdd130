Calgary, Alberta Canada

Calgary is the city where I lived until I was 30. It is a beautiful city when warm and sunny. But those days are pretty rare.

Weather is a large part of Calgary's personality. I have been in Calgary in February and it has been -40 degrees — Celsius and Fahrenheit are the same at -40 (which is why I now live in Arizona).

Calgary is known for the Calgary Stampede and the 1988 Winter Olympics.
